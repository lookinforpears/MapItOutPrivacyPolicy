# Map It Out! Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026
**Developer:** LookinForPears ("we", "us")
**Contact:** lookinforpears@gmail.com

Map It Out! is a map-drawing puzzle game. We built it so it doesn't need to know
who you are. This policy explains what the game stores, where, and who can see it.

## The short version

- We don't collect your personal information, and we don't run any servers that
  receive it.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking in the game.
- Your progress is saved on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud
  account. We can't see it.
- Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

## What the game stores

**Your progress** (explorer name, level, stars, scores, gold and gems, unlocked
items, settings) is saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is
kept in your private iCloud storage so it's restored if you reinstall the game or
use another device. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is
covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We
can't access it.

You can erase your progress at any time in **Settings → Reset Progress**.

## Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game uses it to recognize you between
devices (and, in future updates, for leaderboards and achievements). Game Center
is run by Apple; your Game Center name and any scores you share there are covered
by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## In-app purchases

Gem packs and the Starter Bundle are sold through the App Store. Apple processes
the payment; the game only receives a confirmation that the purchase went
through, which it records on your device so items aren't granted twice. We never
receive your name, payment card or billing details.

## Nearby Play

Nearby Play connects devices on the same Wi-Fi network directly to each other.
To do this, the game asks for permission to use your local network. While you're
in a Nearby game, the other players in that game see your explorer name, level,
profile picture and portrait frame, and your map and scores during the match.
Nothing is sent over the internet or to us, and nothing is kept after the match.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from
anyone, including children. Purchases can be restricted with Apple's Screen Time
and Ask to Buy settings.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for
significant changes, mention it in the game's update notes.
